## Business Problem

Students and aspiring data professionals often struggle to understand the rapidly changing job market. Information about job opportunities, in-demand skills, salary trends, hiring locations, and experience requirements is scattered across multiple platforms, making it difficult to make informed career decisions.

Most job portals focus only on listing vacancies and do not provide analytics that help users understand the overall market. As a result, job seekers frequently spend time learning skills with lower market demand, applying to unsuitable roles, or making career decisions without reliable data.

**TalentPulse AI** addresses this problem by integrating data from multiple public sources to create a centralized job market intelligence platform for Data & AI careers in India. The platform analyzes hiring trends, skill demand, salary patterns, company hiring activity, and geographic opportunities, enabling students and job seekers to make data-driven career decisions rather than relying on guesswork.
