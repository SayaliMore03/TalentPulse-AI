# Project Overview

## Project Title

TalentPulse AI: Career Intelligence Platform for Data Professionals

---

## Project Description

TalentPulse AI is an end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes the Indian Data & AI job market using multiple public datasets. The platform provides insights into hiring trends, in-demand skills, salary patterns, company hiring activity, and geographic opportunities to help students and job seekers make data-driven career decisions.

The project demonstrates the complete analytics lifecycle, including data collection, ETL, data warehousing, SQL analytics, Python-based analysis, and interactive Power BI dashboards.

---

## Business Problem

Students and aspiring data professionals often struggle to understand the rapidly changing job market. Information about job opportunities, in-demand skills, salary trends, hiring locations, and experience requirements is scattered across multiple platforms, making it difficult to make informed career decisions.

Most job portals focus only on listing vacancies and do not provide analytics that help users understand the overall market. As a result, job seekers frequently spend time learning skills with lower market demand, applying to unsuitable roles, or making career decisions without reliable data.

TalentPulse AI addresses this problem by integrating multiple public datasets into a centralized analytics platform that provides actionable insights about the Data & AI job market in India.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze hiring trends for Data & AI roles.
- Identify the most in-demand technical skills.
- Compare salaries across cities, roles, and experience levels.
- Identify companies actively hiring data professionals.
- Build an end-to-end analytics pipeline from raw data to business insights.
- Create interactive Power BI dashboards for career decision-making.
