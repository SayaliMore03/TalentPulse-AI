# Business Requirements

## Purpose

The purpose of TalentPulse AI is to provide students and job seekers with data-driven insights into the Indian Data & AI job market. The platform will analyze publicly available job market data and present meaningful insights to help users make informed career decisions.

---

# Business Goals

- Understand the current job market for Data & AI roles.
- Identify the most in-demand technical skills.
- Compare salaries across different locations and experience levels.
- Identify companies actively hiring data professionals.
- Help users understand hiring trends through interactive dashboards.

---

# Target Users

- Students
- Fresh Graduates
- Job Seekers
- Career Switchers

---

# Business Questions

The platform should answer the following questions:

### Job Market

- How many Data & AI jobs are currently available?
- Which cities have the highest number of openings?
- Which companies are hiring the most?

### Skills

- Which skills appear most frequently in job postings?
- Which tools are most in demand?
- Which skills are commonly required together?

### Salary

- What is the average salary for different roles?
- How does salary vary by city?
- How does salary vary by experience level?

### Experience

- How many jobs are available for freshers?
- What experience level is most commonly required?

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard should display:

- Total Active Jobs
- Average Salary
- Top Hiring Company
- Top Hiring City
- Most In-Demand Skill
- Remote Job Percentage
- Fresher Opportunities
- Average Experience Required

---

# Functional Requirements

The system should be able to:

- Collect job market data from public sources.
- Clean and preprocess raw data.
- Store processed data in a SQL database.
- Perform analytical queries.
- Generate interactive Power BI dashboards.
- Display hiring trends and business insights.

---

# Out of Scope (Version 1)

The following features are not included in Version 1:

- Resume Analyzer
- AI Chatbot
- Course Recommendation
- Job Application Tracker
- Machine Learning Predictions
